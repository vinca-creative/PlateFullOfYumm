If you’ve been searching for the perfect garlic butter broccoli recipe, you’re in the right place! Broccoli and I have a story. Believe it or not, this vibrant green vegetable was once the most hated item on our family’s plate. It sat there, ignored and unloved. But everything changed the day I learned how to cook broccoli perfectly.

Steaming it just right—bright green, tender yet crisp—was the first step. Then came the revelation: sautéing it with aromatics like onions, capsicum, and garlic transforms its flavor completely. Suddenly, broccoli wasn’t boring anymore; it was bursting with life and taste.

Oh, that creamy, perfectly seasoned Greek yogurt base with a hint of garlic—it’s the secret that turns a simple plate into a Plateful of Yum. It’s cooling, tangy, and balances the buttery stir-fry beautifully. I love using homemade yogurt for this sauce because it’s fresh and flavorful. If you’d like to make your own, check out my hassle-free method of making yogurt— it’s foolproof and so easy!

Now, this garlic butter broccoli recipe is a family favorite. I can have it as a light lunch or dinner, paired with tofu, eggs, or chicken. Sometimes, I go for comfort and serve it over a warm bowl of buttered rice. It’s versatile, wholesome, and honestly, a little addictive.

How to Steam Broccoli Perfectly

  1. Prepare the florets: Wash and cut broccoli into bite-sized florets.
  2. Boil water: In a pot, bring about 1 inch of water to a boil.
  3. Set up a steamer: Place a steamer basket over the boiling water.
  4. Steam: Add broccoli to the basket, cover with a lid, and steam for 4-5 minutes until bright green and slightly tender.
  5. Check doneness: Broccoli should be crisp-tender, not mushy. Remove immediately to stop cooking.

Garlic Butter Broccoli Stir-Fry

Yield: 2 servings
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 20 minutes

Garlic Butter Broccoli Stir-Fry with Creamy Yogurt Sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 head broccoli broken down into florets
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced or ½ tsp garlic flakes
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ red capsicum, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Dressing

  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Steam broccoli for 4-5 minutes until bright green and slightly tender.
  2. Heat butter in a pan over low heat. Add garlic cloves and garlic flakes; allow flavors to infuse.
  3. Add onion and capsicum, sprinkle a pinch of salt, and sauté until softened.
  4. Toss in steamed broccoli and stir-fry for 1 minute. Season to taste.
  5. Prepare sauce by mixing Greek yogurt with one clove minced garlic and salt to taste.
  6. To serve, spread 2 tbsp of yogurt sauce in a bowl and top with the stir-fried vegetables.
