Welcome to another delicious and nutritious recipe on my blog, your go-to source for easy, healthy meals. Today, I’m sharing a family favourite: Easy Chicken Curry. This dish comes together in just a few minutes, making it perfect for a quick and satisfying weekday dinner. Packed with protein from the chicken and rich in flavour from caramelized onions and aromatic spices, this chicken curry is not only tasty but also nutritious. Serve it with rice or rotis for a wholesome meal that everyone will love. Plus, it’s versatile enough to fit into a variety of meal plans, whether you’re a busy mom, a working professional, or someone looking for easy recipes that don’t compromise on taste or nutrition. 

Easy Chicken Curry Recipe 

Preparation Time: 15 minutes   

Marinating Time: 30 minutes   

Cooking Time: 15 minutes   

Serving Size: 4 

Ingredients

For marinating: 

  • 500 grams of chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces 
  • Salt to taste 
  • 1/4 teaspoon of black pepper 
  • 1/2 teaspoon of garam masala 
  • 1 teaspoon of chili powder (optional) 

For curry: 

  • 1 cup of caramelized onions 
  • 2 tbsp of coconut oil 
  • 1/4 tsp of turmeric 
  • 2 tsp of coriander seeds 
  • 1 tsp of garam masala 
  • 1 heaped tsp of chili powder (adjust to your taste) 
  • Salt to taste 
  • Curry leaves and coriander leaves for garnish 

Instructions: 

  1. Marinate the Chicken: In a bowl, coat chicken pieces with salt, black pepper, garam masala, and optionally chili powder. Let them marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat the Oven: Next, preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
  3. Roast the Chicken: Once the oven is ready, place the marinated chicken pieces on a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Roast them for 15 minutes, flipping halfway through, until cooked through.
  4. Prepare the Spice Mix: Meanwhile, in a wok, heat 2 tbsp of coconut oil over low heat. Add 1/4 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp coriander seeds, and 1 tsp garam masala. Let the spices warm up for 10 to 15 seconds until aromatic. Turn off the heat, then add chili powder and mix well.
  5. Add Caramelized Onions and Chili Powder: Incorporate 1 cup of caramelized onions into the spice mix and turn on the heat. Mix thoroughly to combine with the spices. For easy caramelized onions, refer to my recipe that can be prepared in just 15 minutes.
  6. Combine with Roast Chicken: Next, add the roasted chicken pieces to the wok. Gently stir until they are fully coated with the spice and onion mixture. Adjust the consistency by adding boiling water if needed.
  7. Season and Garnish: Taste the dish and adjust the salt to your preference. Finish by garnishing with fresh curry leaves and coriander leaves for an extra burst of flavour and colour.

Serving Suggestion: 

Serve this flavourful oven-roasted chicken curry with steamed rice or warm rotis for a complete meal. 

Additional Tips: 

  • Caramelized Onions: Use our easy Instant Pot caramelized onions recipe for a quick and delicious base. You can prepare these onions as part of your meal prep or during the dish preparation itself. 
  • Spice Adjustment: Feel free to adjust the chili powder to suit your heat preference. 

Enjoy this hearty and nutritious oven-roasted chicken curry that brings comfort and flavor to your dinner table!
