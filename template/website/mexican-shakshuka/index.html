Shakshuka, a beloved North African and Middle Eastern breakfast, traditionally consists of poached eggs simmered in a rich tomato sauce with peppers, onions, and spices. It’s a hearty and flavorful meal perfect for breakfast or brunch. I put a fun twist on this classic dish by creating a Mexican shakshuka. This recipe replaces the traditional tomato base with a vibrant and zesty salsa. Not only is this version delicious, but it is also incredibly easy to prepare. As a result, it’s perfect for beginners who want to impress their family and friends.

As a busy mom who’s always on the lookout for quick and nutritious recipes, this easy version of Mexican shakshuka combines the convenience of store-bought salsa with the heartiness of eggs, creating a breakfast that’s both impressive and kid-friendly. So, let’s dive into this vibrant recipe together!

Mexican Shakshuka Recipe

Yield: 2
Prep Time: 2 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 12 minutes

Try Mexican Shakshuka, a tasty twist on the classic dish with poached eggs in a spicy tomato salsa!

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • 4 large eggs 
  • 1 bottle (300g) of your favorite store-bought salsa (choose mild, medium, or spicy) 
  • Salt and pepper, to taste 
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro) leaves, chopped (for garnish) 
  • Spring onions (scallions), thinly sliced (for garnish) 
  • Wholesome bread or buns (to serve)

Instructions

  1. Heat a skillet or frying pan over medium heat.  
  2. Then, melt a tablespoon of butter in the pan.  
  3. Pour the entire bottle of salsa into the skillet, and season it with a pinch of salt and pepper. 
  4.  Use a spoon to create four small wells in the salsa.  
  5. Next, gently crack open 4 eggs, one into each well, spacing them evenly.  
  6. Cover the skillet with a lid and let the eggs cook for about 5-7 minutes, ensuring the whites set while the yolks remain slightly runny. If you prefer well-done eggs, cook them a bit longer.  
  7. Sprinkle additional salt and pepper over the eggs before removing the skillet from the heat. 
  8.  Garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves and spring onions, then serve the shakshuka directly from the skillet, family-style.  
  9. Finally, don’t forget to have some wholesome bread or buns on the side for dipping! 

Why Moms Love This Recipe: 

  • Quick and Easy: With just a few ingredients and minimal prep, this shakshuka comes together effortlessly. 
  • Kid-Friendly: The vibrant colors and familiar flavors make it appealing to little ones. 
  • Nutritious: Eggs provide protein, while salsa adds a dose of veggies and spices. 
  • Impressive Presentation: Serve it in the skillet for that rustic, homey feel. 

Enjoy this Mexican-inspired shakshuka, and watch your family devour it with smiles! 🌶️🍳🥖
