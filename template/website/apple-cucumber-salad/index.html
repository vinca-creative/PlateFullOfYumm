Apple and Cucumber Salad is a delightful recipe perfect for when you crave something light and refreshing. The crispiness of apples with the cool crunch of cucumbers is enhanced by a touch of spicy jalapeños and a hint of lemon. This salad is not just a treat for your taste buds but also a powerhouse of nutrients.  Let’s dive into the recipe!

Apple Cucumber Salad recipe

Yield: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 10 minutes

Find out how to make the refreshing apple cucumber salad! Spiced with jalapeños and a hint of lemon juice, this vibrant dish is perfect as a light meal or a side dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 green apple, diced
  • 1 red apple, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 5 pieces of pickled jalapeños, chopped (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 tablespoon of lemon juice
  • Coriander leaves for garnish
  • A few salt flakes

Instructions

  1. First, dice the green apple, red apple, and cucumber into small pieces. Ensure they are of similar size for a uniform texture. 
  2. Next, combine the diced apples and cucumber in a large bowl. 
  3. Then, chop the pickled jalapeños into small pieces and add them to the bowl. Adjust the quantity based on your spice preference. 
  4. After that, pour in the lemon juice and toss everything together gently to ensure even coating. 
  5. Garnish with coriander leaves over the salad for a fresh aroma and flavor. 
  6. Finally, add a few salt flakes just before serving to enhance the flavors. 

Notes

Customization: You can easily customize the apple and cucumber salad to suit your taste. For a sweeter version, add a drizzle of honey or maple syrup. If you prefer more crunch, add some chopped nuts like walnuts or almonds. 

Serving Suggestions: This salad pairs wonderfully with grilled chicken or fish. Last time I made this recipe, I served this as a side dish for our family Nachos Night!

Health Benefits: Apples are rich in fiber and vitamin C, while cucumbers provide hydration and essential nutrients. Jalapeños add a metabolismboosting kick, and lemon juice is a great source of vitamin C. 

This Apple and Cucumber Salad is a delightful, nutritious dish that’s easy to prepare and packed with flavors. It’s perfect for those who love fresh, vibrant meals. The combination of sweet, spicy, and tangy elements makes it a standout dish for any occasion. Whether you’re looking for a healthy snack or a refreshing side dish, this salad will not disappoint.

Try this recipe today and let the crisp and refreshing taste of Apple and Cucumber Salad brighten up your meal. Don’t forget to share your experience and any customizations you tried in the comments below. Happy cooking!
