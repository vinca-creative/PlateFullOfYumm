Welcome to Plate Full of Yum, where we celebrate delicious and wholesome recipes that make you feel good from the inside out! Today, I am sharing one of my favorite treats: the Rich Banana and Cacao Smoothie. Whether you’re looking for a nutritious breakfast, a post-workout snack, or simply a delicious indulgence, this smoothie is the perfect choice. 

With just a few wholesome ingredients, you can create a drink that’s both satisfying and nourishing. The key ingredient, frozen banana, gives the smoothie a thick and creamy texture. Paired with cacao powder, it transforms into a chocolatey treat that is both decadent and nutritious. Plus, the addition of rolled oats not only adds a wonderful texture but also helps keep you full and satisfied. 

What makes this smoothie so special is its versatility. You can easily customize it to meet your dietary preferences and nutritional needs. For instance, you can make it vegan, boost its protein content, or adjust the sweetness to your liking. Each variation offers a unique twist on the base recipe, allowing you to enjoy this smoothie in different ways.  

Customization Options 

Boost Your Protein Intake: If you want to turn this smoothie into a more substantial meal, consider adding a protein boost. You can mix in a scoop of your favorite protein powder, such as whey protein or a plant-based option like pea protein. Another great option is to stir in a tablespoon of chia seeds or flaxseeds, which not only increase protein but also add healthy fats and fiber. 

Make it Sweeter: If you prefer a sweeter smoothie, you have several options. Simply add a teaspoon of honey or maple syrup to the blender for a natural sweetener. Alternatively, you can use dates for a more subtle sweetness. If you’re vegan, opt for maple syrup or agave nectar as sweeteners. 

Vegan and Dairy-Free Options: For a vegan version of this smoothie, just replace the milk with a plantbased alternative. Almond milk, soy milk, or oat milk all work wonderfully and complement the banana and cacao flavors. Additionally, you can use nondairy yogurt for an extra creamy texture without any animal products. 

Add Extra Nutrients: To make your smoothie even more nutritious, consider adding a handful of spinach or kale. These greens blend seamlessly into the smoothie, offering a boost of vitamins and minerals without altering the flavor. You could also add a tablespoon of nut butter for additional protein and healthy fats. 

Enhance the Chocolate Flavor: If you love a rich chocolate flavor, you can increase the amount of cacao powder to 2 tablespoons or add a tablespoon of cocoa powder. You might also mix in a few dark chocolate chips or a sprinkle of cocoa nibs for added texture and indulgence. 

Frozen Fruit Variations: For a refreshing twist, try adding other frozen fruits along with the banana. Frozen blueberries can add a burst of flavor and additional nutrients. Simply blend these fruits with the banana for a smoothie that’s both diverse and delicious. 

Banana Cacao Smoothie Recipe

Yield: 1
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Total Time: 5 minutes

This rich and creamy Banana Cacao Smoothie is easy to make and perfect for breakfast or a snack. Customize with protein boosts, vegan options, and more for a delicious, healthy treat.

Ingredients

  • 1 banana (preferably frozen)
  • 1 cup milk of choice (dairy or nondairy)
  • 1/4 cup rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon cacao powder

Instructions

    1. Combine all ingredients in a blender. 
    2. Blend until smooth and creamy. 
    3. Pour into a glass and enjoy immediately.
