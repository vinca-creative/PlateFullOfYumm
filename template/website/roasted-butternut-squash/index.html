Roasted Butternut Squash

Yield: 4 serves
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Total Time: 25 minutes

This roasted butternut squash recipe is cozy, crispy, garlicky, and easy to make with simple pantry ingredients in just 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium butternut squash
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cut squash into wedges or crescent shapes.
  3. Toss with oil and seasonings.
  4. Arrange on baking tray in a single layer.
  5. Roast for 15 minutes until tender and golden.
  6. Serve warm.

Why Roasted Butternut Squash Fits Perfectly in Balanced Bites

Roasted butternut squash is exactly the kind of recipe that belongs in a Balanced Bites collection because it combines nourishment, simplicity, comfort, and versatility in one easy dish.

Here’s why it fits so well:

  • Whole-food ingredients: The recipe uses simple, minimally processed ingredients like butternut squash, olive oil, and seasonings.
  • Nutrient-dense: Butternut squash provides fiber, vitamin A, vitamin C, and antioxidants while still feeling cozy and satisfying.
  • Meal-prep friendly: Balanced eating becomes easier when recipes can be prepared ahead and reused in salads, grain bowls, soups, or side dishes.
  • Family-friendly: The caramelized sweetness appeals to both adults and kids, making healthy eating feel approachable instead of restrictive. Even picky eaters love it!
  • Flexible for different lifestyles: It naturally fits vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free eating patterns.

Health Benefits of Roasted Butternut Squash

Butternut squash is packed with nutrients that support overall wellness while adding incredible flavor to meals.

According to Healthline, butternut squash is rich in:

  • Vitamin A, which supports eye health and immune function
  • Vitamin C, which helps support collagen production and immunity
  • Fiber, which promotes digestion and fullness
  • Antioxidants that may help protect cells from oxidative stress

Meanwhile, Harvard Health notes that colorful vegetables like squash contain plant compounds associated with long-term health benefits when included as part of a balanced diet.

Olive oil also provides heart-healthy fats, while garlic contains beneficial plant compounds that contribute flavor and nutrition.

Because this recipe is roasted instead of heavily fried, it keeps the dish light while still delivering rich flavor and satisfying texture.
