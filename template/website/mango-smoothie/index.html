Welcome back to Plate Full of Yum, your go-to blog for delicious, nutritious, and wholesome recipes that the entire family will love. Today, I’m thrilled to share a recipe that has become an absolute favorite in my household—Mango Smoothie. This delightful smoothie is not only incredibly easy to make but also adored by my kids. It is a perfect choice for breakfast, an after-school snack, or even a healthy dessert. 

This Mango Smoothie is a breeze to whip up with just three simple ingredients: frozen mango, Greek yogurt, and milk. The vibrant flavor of the mango pairs perfectly with the Greek yogurt and milk. The result is an amazing drink that is both satisfying and refreshing.

Whether you need a quick breakfast option, an after-school snack, or a guilt-free dessert, this smoothie is the deal. Plus, it’s a fantastic way to sneak some extra nutrients into your family’s diet without any fuss. So, let’s get blending and enjoy this delicious Mango Smoothie!

Customizations for Mango Smoothie

Milk Variations: You can easily swap regular milk with almond milk, oat milk, soy milk, or coconut milk to suit your dietary needs and preferences. 

Yogurt: Swap the regular yogurt to plant-based yogurt for a vegan option.

Protein Boost: Add a scoop of your favorite protein powder to transform this smoothie into a protein-packed treat, perfect for post-workout recovery. 

Sweeteners: If you prefer a sweeter smoothie, add a touch of honey or maple syrup

Mango Smoothie Recipe

Yield: 2
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Total Time: 5 minutes

This kid-friendly Mango Smoothie is a delicious and nutritious drink that's perfect for breakfast, an after-school snack, or a healthy dessert. Made with just three ingredients—frozen mango, Greek yogurt, and milk—it's incredibly easy to whip up and packed with vitamins and protein. Enjoy this refreshing smoothie any time of the day!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Mango frozen
  • 1 cup Milk your preferred type of milk
  • 1/2 cup Greek Yogurt diary or diary free

Instructions

    1. Add the frozen mango chunks, Greek yogurt, and milk to a blender.
    2. Blend the ingredients on high until the mixture is smooth and creamy. You may need to stop and scrape down the sides of the blender. 
    3. If the smoothie is too thick, add a bit more milk and blend to get the desired consistency.
    4. Pour into glasses and enjoy!
