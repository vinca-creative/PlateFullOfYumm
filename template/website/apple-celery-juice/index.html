Yesterday, was Onam and we celebrated it with a grand feast! After indulging in a lavish Sadya, my digestive system craved a break. I found my body needed something light and hydrating. That’s when I turned to this simple and refreshing Apple Celery Juice. It’s the perfect reset, offering a gentle cleanse while still tasting delicious.

Why I Love Apple Celery Juice? This juice combines the fresh, crisp flavors of green apple and celery. A dash of lemon gives it an extra zing too. Not only does this drink taste great, but it also offers key health benefits that support digestion and overall wellness.

Health Benefits of Celery and Apple Juice:

  1. Digestive Support: Celery’s rich fiber and water content help promote healthy digestion and prevent bloating. After a heavy meal, this juice helps relieve discomfort.
  2. Hydration Boost: The water content in celery and apple is incredibly hydrating. Adding tender coconut water adds a boost of electrolytes to this juice.
  3. Rich in Antioxidants: Green apples are packed with antioxidants, especially vitamin C, which helps detoxify body and strengthen immune system.
  4. Low in Calories, High in Nutrients: This juice is low in calories but rich in essential vitamins and minerals, making it a light yet nourishing option to reset after a heavy meal.

Celery and Apple Juice

Yield: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 10 minutes

Let try this simple and refreshing Celery and Apple Juice. It’s the perfect reset, offering a gentle cleanse while tasting delicious.

Ingredients

  • 1 green apple, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 3 cups of water or coconut water
  • A dash of lemon juice (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar (optional, for sweetness)

Instructions

    1. Blend all the ingredients in a blender.
    2. Strain the juice using a cheese cloth or strainer and discard the pulp.
    3. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.
Nutrition Information:
Yield: 4 cups Serving Size: 250 ml
Amount Per Serving: Calories: 60Total Fat: 0gSodium: 50mgFiber: 2gSugar: 12g

Customizations

This juice is incredibly versatile. Here’s how we can customize it:

  • Add Ginger: A small piece of fresh ginger gives the juice a warm, spicy kick and boosts its anti-inflammatory properties.
  • Boost with Greens: To add extra nutrients, throw in a handful of spinach or kale. It doesn’t affect the flavor much but gives the juice an extra vitamin boost.
  • Swap Water for Coconut Water: Using coconut water as the base adds natural sweetness and extra hydration through its electrolytes.
  • Sweetness Adjustment: If you want a sweeter juice, use a tablespoon of honey, maple syrup, or agave nectar instead of sugar.
